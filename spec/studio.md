# Teacher studio

`/teachers/studio`. A recording studio for guitar lessons. Design: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-19-teacher-studio-design.md`.

## Now
- **Teacher area:** `/teachers` links to the studio. A login will wrap it later.
- **Browsers:** desktop Chrome and Edge, at least 1024 px wide. Anywhere else the page says "The studio needs desktop Chrome or Edge."
- **Layout:** the Add a widget button and the Layers list in a narrow left column; the 16:9 frame takes the rest (exactly what a recording will show), as wide as it can be without getting taller than the window.
- **Layers:** every widget in the frame, top first. Each row selects its widget and has hide/show, bring forward, send backward and delete.
- **Palette:** `/` or ⌘K (or Add a widget) opens the widget picker over the page (never in the frame). Before anything is typed it lists every widget, by section with smaller subsection headings: **Key and sound** (Key & scale, Metronome, Tuner), **Scale** (Notes: Scale notes, Modes with the same notes; On the fretboard: Fretboard, Fretboard positions; Pentatonic boxes 1–5; Scale positions 1–5; 3 notes per string 1–7), **Chords** (In the key: Chords in the key, Triads, Arpeggios, Progressions; Sus & extensions: one circle diagram per chord), **CAGED** (The whole neck: CAGED overview, CAGED fretboard; One shape: C, A, G, E, D), **Theory** (Circle of fifths, Piano keys, Improvisation, Tunings), **Cheatsheet pages** (every cheatsheet page in full, as on `/cheatsheet`), **Brand** (Logo, Site address) and **Studio** (camera and screen-share commands). A widget on the stage has its row ticked, with a green outline: click a row (or Enter) to put it on the stage or take it off; the picker stays open, so several can be ticked at once. A command runs and closes it. Escape or a click outside closes. Typing searches names and other words (`fretboard`, `metronome`, `drone`, `tuner`, `triads page`, `same notes`, `e shape`, `a minor c shape`, `sus iv`, `pent pos 2`, `c major 3nps box 2`, `camera corner`); a key (`a dorian`) or a mode alone (`dorian`, that mode on the master root) lists the widgets that follow a key, in it. Search keeps its best result first and orders the sections by their best result. There is no single-chord widget (the Chords page has a chord search), and no compact Chords or Modes panel (Chords in the key and Modes with the same notes show the same, with chord shapes).
- **Widgets:** names say what the widget is and its scale, never the master root (`Fretboard · Dorian`, `CAGED E shape (root on low E) · minor`); a root pinned on the widget shows (`Fretboard · G Dorian`). The **Key & scale** widget is the master: the root (or none), the scale family, the mode and the note labels. Every widget added takes the master key and scale and follows it. On hover, each key-aware widget shows two tags in its top-left corner, its root and its scale (`data-chrome`); changing one pins that part on the widget, so the master no longer moves it: a widget pinned to Dorian keeps Dorian when the master scale changes, and still moves to a new master root, so A Dorian and A Phrygian side by side both become C Dorian and C Phrygian. A key picked inside a widget (a mode in Modes with the same notes) pins the same way; a key typed in the search is pinned where it differs from the master. A pinned widget's tags have **Follow** to go back to the master. The Metronome follows the root only. CAGED widgets use the minor shapes when their key's third is minor. A whole cheatsheet page, and Chords in the key, fit the widget's width and scroll inside it. Every widget has a delete button in its top-right corner on hover (`data-chrome`; camera and screen share keep their own close). The Tuner is the `/tuner` tuner (no microphone until Start). Brand widgets: the attunr logo (top right) and the site address (bottom centre), each on a dark pill, each at most once; the Key & scale widget and the Tuner exist at most once too. A stage saved with the old single Sus and extensions section opens with the first chord's diagram; a saved chord box still shows.
- **Moving:** drag a widget (from its top strip at once, from anywhere after a few pixels, so clicks still play), resize from any of its four corner dots (the opposite corner stays put). Selecting a widget brings it to the top (camera and screen share keep their place under the widgets). The outline and dots show only on hover or when selected.
- **Zoom:** hold ⌘ and scroll, or pinch on the trackpad, to zoom into a widget's content around the pointer (up to 6×); a plain scroll pans a zoomed widget; `0` resets the selected one. The widget's box stays the same size; the zoom is saved with the layer.
- **Add a widget:** the button at the top left (`/` or ⌘K) opens the palette. **Keys** (all of them with `?`): `/` or ⌘K add, `C` camera (minimized), `F` fullscreen or minimize the camera, ⇧F the same for the screen share, arrows move (Shift: more), ⌥+arrows resize, `0` reset zoom, ⌫ delete, Esc deselect. None fire while typing in a field.
- **Camera:** a layer like any other: drag, resize, reorder, hide or delete it. The palette offers whichever applies: **Camera** (adds it, minimized), **Fullscreen camera** / **Minimize camera** (whichever it is not now; minimized = the bottom-right portrait corner) and **Close camera**. Hovering the camera shows the same fullscreen/minimize and close buttons in its top-right corner (never recorded). It never opens on its own: the layer shows "Start camera" until clicked (also after a reload). Refused or unplugged, it says "Camera off: click to retry". With more than one camera, a picker shows under the Layers list. Video only; the camera's choice is remembered in `musickit.studio.camera`.
- **Screen share:** **Screen share** asks Chrome for a window or screen and adds it as a layer at the bottom, full frame, shown whole (letterboxed). Then the palette offers **Fullscreen screen share** / **Minimize screen share** (minimized = bottom left) and **Close screen share**, and hovering it shows the same corner buttons. No sound. Stopping it from Chrome's bar or closing or deleting the layer ends it. After a reload the layer asks to share again.
- **Saved:** the stage (layers, their place and size as fractions of the frame, the Key widget's key) is saved in `musickit.studio.stage` and comes back on the next visit.
