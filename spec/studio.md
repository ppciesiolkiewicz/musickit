# Teacher studio

`/teachers/studio`. A recording studio for guitar lessons. Design: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-19-teacher-studio-design.md`.

## Now
- **Teacher area:** `/teachers` links to the studio. A login will wrap it later.
- **Browsers:** desktop Chrome and Edge, at least 1024 px wide. Anywhere else the page says "The studio needs desktop Chrome or Edge."
- **Layout:** the Add a widget button and the Layers list in a narrow left column; the 16:9 frame takes the rest (exactly what a recording will show), as wide as it can be without getting taller than the window.
- **Layers:** every widget in the frame, top first. Each row selects its widget and has hide/show, bring forward, send backward and delete.
- **Palette:** `/` or ⌘K opens a search over the page (never in the frame). It understands chords (`e6`), keys (`a dorian`), positions (`e minor pent pos 1`, `c major 3nps box 2`), theory panels (`circle g`, `caged`), the scale fretboard (`fretboard`, `g major fretboard`), CAGED (`caged` gives the panel, the CAGED fretboard and the five boxes; `caged fretboard`; `e shape`, `a minor c shape`), the Drone section (`drone`, `drone a dorian`), scale sections (`every chord g`, `same notes`, `on guitar`), the sus and extension diagrams (`sus` gives one per chord, `sus iv` one), a mode with no root (`dorian`, `phrygian fretboard`: that mode on the stage's root, for comparing modes), the Key widget (`key`), the logo (`logo`, `brand`), the site address (`url`, `site`, `brand`) and the camera and screen-share commands (below). ↑ ↓ Enter picks (the list scrolls to keep the pick in view), Escape closes. Results sit under a separator per category: Key and theory, Scale, Positions, CAGED, Chords, Sound, Brand, Studio (search orders the categories by their best result). Before anything is typed it lists every widget there is (recent picks first, under Recent): the Key widget and every theory panel, the scale panel, the scale fretboard, the scale sections and the seven sus and extension diagrams, a pentatonic box, a scale position and a 3-notes-per-string position, the CAGED panel, the CAGED fretboard and each of the five boxes, the key's first chord, the Drone section, the logo, the site address, the camera and screen share.
- **Widgets:** a chord box (click to strum, ‹ › for other shapes), any cheatsheet panel in its own key, a scale position on the neck, the scale fretboard (the scale on the whole neck), the CAGED fretboard (the five boxes up the neck), one CAGED box ("E shape, root on low E": its chord, arpeggio, pentatonic and scale in a row), one chord's sus and extension circle diagram (one widget per chord of the key), the scale page's Drone section (click, drone, drums, bass), scale-page sections, the Key widget (the twelve root notes and the note labels; no scale or mode), and two brand widgets: the attunr logo (top right) and the site address (bottom centre), each on a dark pill so it reads over the camera, each at most once. Each widget keeps its own key. The Key widget moves every widget except chord boxes to its root note and keeps each widget's scale or mode, so A Dorian and A Phrygian side by side both become C Dorian and C Phrygian. CAGED widgets use the minor shapes when their key's third is minor. A stage saved with the old single Sus and extensions section opens with the first chord's diagram.
- **Moving:** drag a widget (from its top strip at once, from anywhere after a few pixels, so clicks still play), resize from any of its four corner dots (the opposite corner stays put). Selecting a widget brings it to the top (camera and screen share keep their place under the widgets). The outline and dots show only on hover or when selected.
- **Zoom:** hold ⌘ and scroll, or pinch on the trackpad, to zoom into a widget's content around the pointer (up to 6×); a plain scroll pans a zoomed widget; `0` resets the selected one. The widget's box stays the same size; the zoom is saved with the layer.
- **Add a widget:** the button at the top left (`/` or ⌘K) opens the palette. **Keys** (all of them with `?`): `/` or ⌘K add, `C` camera (minimized), `F` fullscreen or minimize the camera, ⇧F the same for the screen share, arrows move (Shift: more), ⌥+arrows resize, `0` reset zoom, ⌫ delete, Esc deselect. None fire while typing in a field.
- **Camera:** a layer like any other: drag, resize, reorder, hide or delete it. The palette offers whichever applies: **Camera** (adds it, minimized), **Fullscreen camera** / **Minimize camera** (whichever it is not now; minimized = the bottom-right portrait corner) and **Close camera**. Hovering the camera shows the same fullscreen/minimize and close buttons in its top-right corner (never recorded). It never opens on its own: the layer shows "Start camera" until clicked (also after a reload). Refused or unplugged, it says "Camera off: click to retry". With more than one camera, a picker shows under the Layers list. Video only; the camera's choice is remembered in `musickit.studio.camera`.
- **Screen share:** **Screen share** asks Chrome for a window or screen and adds it as a layer at the bottom, full frame, shown whole (letterboxed). Then the palette offers **Fullscreen screen share** / **Minimize screen share** (minimized = bottom left) and **Close screen share**, and hovering it shows the same corner buttons. No sound. Stopping it from Chrome's bar or closing or deleting the layer ends it. After a reload the layer asks to share again.
- **Saved:** the stage (layers, their place and size as fractions of the frame, the Key widget's key) is saved in `musickit.studio.stage` and comes back on the next visit.
