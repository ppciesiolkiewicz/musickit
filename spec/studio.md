# Teacher studio

`/teachers/studio`. A recording studio for guitar lessons. Design: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-19-teacher-studio-design.md`.

## Now
- **Teacher area:** `/teachers` links to the studio. A login will wrap it later.
- **Browsers:** desktop Chrome and Edge, at least 1024 px wide. Anywhere else the page says "The studio needs desktop Chrome or Edge."
- **Layout:** the Add a widget button and the Layers list in a narrow left column; the 16:9 frame takes the rest (exactly what a recording will show), as wide as it can be without getting taller than the window.
- **Layers:** every widget in the frame, newest first. Each row selects its widget and has pin, hide/show, bring forward, send backward and delete. The chevron by **Add a widget** folds the panel to a narrow strip: unfold, add, and per widget a letter or two of its name (its full name on hover; click selects it, a hidden one is dimmed) and delete. The fold is remembered in `musickit.studio.panelFolded`. A **page** tag marks the bigger widgets (a whole cheatsheet page, Fretboard positions, Chords in the key) in the palette and the Layers list, as **control** marks Key & scale, the Metronome, the Tuner and the Audio input, and a rose **mic** tag the widgets the audio input lights notes on (fretboards, scale positions, chord boxes, sus & extension diagrams, Fretboard positions, Chords in the key, the compact CAGED panel, and the Scale, Modes, Chords, Progressions and CAGED pages; `takesMic`).
- **Palette:** `/` or ⌘K (or Add a widget) opens the widget picker over the page (never in the frame). Before anything is typed it lists every widget, by section with smaller subsection headings: **Key and sound** (Key & scale, Metronome, Tuner, Audio input), **Scale** (Notes: Scale notes, Modes with the same notes; On the fretboard: Fretboard, Fretboard positions; Pentatonic boxes 1–5; Scale positions 1–5; 3 notes per string 1–7), **Chords** (In the key: Chords in the key, Triads, Arpeggios, Progressions; Sus & extensions: one circle diagram per chord), **CAGED** (The whole neck: CAGED overview, CAGED fretboard; One shape: C, A, G, E, D), **Theory** (Circle of fifths, Piano keys, Improvisation, Tunings), **Cheatsheet pages** (every cheatsheet page in full, as on `/cheatsheet`), **Brand** (Logo, Site address) and **Studio** (camera and screen-share commands). Controls (Key & scale, Metronome, Tuner, Audio input: they set, play or listen to something rather than show it) carry a small amber `control` badge, here and in the Layers list. A widget on the stage has its row ticked, with a green outline: click a row (or Enter) to put it on the stage or take it off; the picker stays open, so several can be ticked at once. A command runs and closes it. Escape or a click outside closes. Typing searches names and other words (`fretboard`, `metronome`, `drone`, `tuner`, `triads page`, `same notes`, `e shape`, `a minor c shape`, `sus iv`, `pent pos 2`, `c major 3nps box 2`, `camera corner`); a key (`a dorian`) or a mode alone (`dorian`, that mode on the master root) lists the widgets that follow a key, in it. Search keeps its best result first and orders the sections by their best result. There is no single-chord widget (the Chords page has a chord search), and no compact Chords or Modes panel (Chords in the key and Modes with the same notes show the same, with chord shapes).
- **Widgets:** names say what the widget is and its scale, never the master root (`Fretboard · Dorian`, `CAGED E shape (root on low E) · minor`); a root pinned on the widget shows (`Fretboard · G Dorian`). The **Key & scale** widget is the master: the root (or none), the scale family, the mode and the note labels. Every widget added takes the master key and scale and follows it. On hover, each key-aware widget shows two tags in its top-left corner, its root and its scale (`data-chrome`); changing one pins that part on the widget, so the master no longer moves it: a widget pinned to Dorian keeps Dorian when the master scale changes, and still moves to a new master root, so A Dorian and A Phrygian side by side both become C Dorian and C Phrygian. A key picked inside a widget (a mode in Modes with the same notes) pins the same way; a key typed in the search is pinned where it differs from the master. A pinned widget's tags have **Follow** to go back to the master. The Metronome follows the root only. CAGED widgets use the minor shapes when their key's third is minor. A whole cheatsheet page, and Chords in the key, fit the widget's width and scroll inside it. Every widget is an opaque dark card, so a widget over another hides it (the logo and site address stay see-through over the picture). On hover every widget shows a small toolbar (`data-chrome`) just outside its top-right corner, so it never covers the widget: above it, or below it when the widget touches the frame's top, inside the corner only when it fills the frame's height (and always for the brand marks). It has pin, delete (camera and screen share keep their own close) and **Fill the frame**: the widget, brought to the top, as big as the frame allows in its own shape (the whole frame when shapes are not kept), centred; the same button (**Back to its place**) returns it. Moving or resizing a filling widget keeps it where it is put. Brand marks have no fill button. The Tuner is the `/tuner` tuner on the studio's audio input: no microphone or input picker of its own, and its Start / Stop turns the audio input on and off. **Pin** (in the hover toolbar, lit while pinned; also in each Layers row) keeps a widget over every widget that is not pinned, whatever is raised or selected, and is saved with the stage. The studio has **one audio input**, and it feeds every widget. The **Audio input** widget (at most once; saved as kind `mic`) shows it. It has two sources, as in the looper: **Guitar** (by default Input 1 of the device) and **Voice** (by default both channels). A switch on the widget picks which one listens. The cog opens a dialog: each source's device (or the site's input from the devices menu) and channel (Input 1, Input 2, Both), **Use this** to switch, and the output list. The setup is kept in `musickit.studio.audioInput`. Picking the widget in the palette starts listening, and Listen / Stop toggles it; a stage opened with one waits for Listen. The widget shows the note heard and how far off it is, with the tuner's compact meter (needle, Hz, ± cents). The input belongs to the studio, not the widget: hiding the widget keeps it listening; it stops when neither it nor the Tuner is left. The heard note lights up (a blue ring, on every place that note falls) on the pinned widgets and the selected one: fretboards, positions and chord boxes, including inside cheatsheet panels. Brand widgets: the attunr logo and the site address, each on a dark pill, each at most once, in fixed places in the top-right corner (the address under the logo): they cannot be moved, resized or zoomed, only shown or hidden (ticked in the palette, the eye in Layers, or the ✕); a stage saved with one elsewhere puts it back; the Key & scale widget and the Tuner exist at most once too. A stage saved with the old single Sus and extensions section opens with the first chord's diagram; a saved chord box still shows.
- **Moving:** drag a widget (from its top strip at once, from anywhere after a few pixels, so clicks still play), resize from any of its four corner dots (the opposite corner stays put). Selecting a widget (a click, a drag, its Layers row) brings it to the top (camera and screen share keep their place under the widgets). Stacking is each layer's own `z`, apart from the Layers list: the list keeps the order widgets were added (newest first), and bringing forward or sending backward changes only the stacking. A stage saved before `z` stacks in list order. The outline and dots show only on hover or when selected. **Keep widget shapes** (a switch under the Layers list, on by default; its tooltip explains it): each widget's frame takes the shape of what it shows, so there is no empty margin around it, and a corner drag keeps that shape (whichever way the corner moved more sets the size). Off, a frame can be any shape and the widget sits centred in it. The choice is remembered in `musickit.studio.keepShape`. A widget whose content is wider than its design width is scaled down in whole, never cut off.
- **Zoom:** hold ⌘ and scroll, or pinch on the trackpad, to zoom into a widget's content around the pointer (up to 6×); a plain scroll pans a zoomed widget; `0` resets the selected one. The widget's box stays the same size; the zoom is saved with the layer.
- **Add a widget:** the button at the top left (`/` or ⌘K) opens the palette. **Keys** (all of them with `?`): `/` or ⌘K add, `C` camera (minimized), `F` fullscreen or minimize the camera, ⇧F the same for the screen share, arrows move (Shift: more), ⌥+arrows resize, `0` reset zoom, ⌫ delete, Esc deselect. None fire while typing in a field.
- **Camera:** a layer like any other: drag, resize, reorder, hide or delete it. The palette offers whichever applies: **Camera** (adds it, minimized), **Fullscreen camera** / **Minimize camera** (whichever it is not now; minimized = the bottom-right portrait corner) and **Close camera**. Hovering the camera shows the same fullscreen/minimize and close buttons in its top-right corner (never recorded). It never opens on its own: the layer shows "Start camera" until clicked (also after a reload). Refused or unplugged, it says "Camera off: click to retry". With more than one camera, a picker shows under the Layers list. Video only; the camera's choice is remembered in `musickit.studio.camera`.
- **Screen share:** **Screen share** asks Chrome for a window or screen and adds it as a layer at the bottom, full frame, shown whole (letterboxed). Then the palette offers **Fullscreen screen share** / **Minimize screen share** (minimized = bottom left) and **Close screen share**, and hovering it shows the same corner buttons. No sound. Stopping it from Chrome's bar or closing or deleting the layer ends it. After a reload the layer asks to share again.
- **Melody and countdown:** two more subject kinds, `melody` (optional `beats` across, 2 to 16) and `countdown`, exist for widget videos (`videos/*.json`), not for the studio: the palette never offers them. They follow a host timeline (`TimelineContext`) and draw nothing without one.
- **Saved:** the stage (layers, their place and size as fractions of the frame, the Key widget's key) is saved in `musickit.studio.stage` and comes back on the next visit.
