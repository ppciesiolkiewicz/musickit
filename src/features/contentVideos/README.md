# Content videos

Videos the app shows players with a tool: a video for a tool at the steps it covers, or a curated block's own video for one of its tool cells. They are made by people with the `content` permission (today the riff maker saves a take as its block's video, with this feature's recorder) and watched by anyone, signed in or not. A plan's day shows them (below), and the Tools videos page (`/admin/content/videos`) lists every tool and level with its video. Videos are recorded on the record page (`/admin/content/videos/record`) and, for a riff, on the riff maker (`/admin/content/riff`), with the same recorder (`ui/RecordVideo.tsx`); the record page sends a riff block's cell there (`?riff=<block id>`). Each exercise opened in the library has a video strip under its tools. A video of the guitar play-along or the melody singer plays with the tool drawn over it, in step with the hands (the overlay, below).

This is not `src/features/videos` (a player's or teacher's own takes, per account, in notes) and not `VIDEO_LIBRARY` or the `video` tool, which stay as they are. It shares only the pure upload rules (`videos/server/rules`), the recorder's pure parts (`videos/recording`, `videos/ref`) and the Range streaming (`videos/server/stream`).

## What a content video is

- A row of `content_videos` (migration `drizzle/0046_content_videos.sql`) and a file in the private Blob store at `content/videos/<made_for>/<id>.<webm|mp4>`. `made_for` is `tool:<tool key>` or a curated block's id (a riff take: the plain block id). The browser picks the id (`crypto.randomUUID()`).
- A **tool key** is the tool's type; a `guitar-play-along` is one tool per shape, `guitar-play-along:<shape>`.
- A **step** is `<ladder id>:<key>` (`picking-drill:beginner`, `chord-changes:1`), from `LADDERS` in `@/features/plan/model/ladders`. `none` is **No level**: a laddered tool's cell that reads no step. A tool with no ladder has no steps (`steps` null).
- **One video per tool and step.** A tool video is saved for the steps ticked; saving takes those steps off the tool's other videos in the same transaction (for a tool with no ladder, the tool itself). A video left with no steps keeps its row and file, with `tool` and `steps` both null. There is no any-step video and no borrowing from a nearby step.
- **A block's own video** lives in `blocks.videos` (`{ cells: { "<n>": "<video id>" } }`), by the cell's index among the block's tool cells in reading order (a section's cells in its place; `toolCells`). Only a curated block has one. Its row has `tool` and `steps` null.

## Which video a tool cell shows

`model/lookup.ts`: the block's own video for that cell (`from: "block"`), else the tool's video whose steps hold the cell's step (`from: "tool"`; a cell with no step matches a video with `steps` null), else none. A video met again at a later cell of the same block is `repeat: true`. A cell whose video's row is gone is left out.

## In a plan's day

Every day of a plan (`DayPanel` in `plan/ui/WeekView.tsx`: today's plan, a past day, a shared plan, a teacher's week, a demo) looks its videos up each time it is drawn (`plan/ui/useDayVideos.ts`), so a video saved today shows in plans made before it. One `lookup` request for the whole day: each exercise is a block (`key` `e<index>`; `blockId` only for a curated exercise, `isCuratedExercise`), its cells the tools **as drawn now** (`model/drawn.ts` `drawnAsks`: the version's chips at `at`, the choice chips at `picks`), so a chip that changes a tool's level changes the video. Each card holds its chips and reports its tools when they move (`ExerciseCard` `onAsks`); a card not on screen counts as first drawn. A video shows only at its **first place in the day** (`dayVideos`: exercises in order, cells in order), whatever the server's per-block `repeat` says. Answers are kept by request, so a chip moved back asks nothing, and the last answer stays while a new one is out.

A tool with a video gets **Watch video** right above it (`ui/CellVideo.tsx`, through `BlockBody`'s `beforeTool`): it opens the video in place, above the tool, which stays on screen and playable; **Hide video** closes it. Nothing loads until it is opened. A file that won't play says "The video could not be played. Check your connection, then press Watch video again." A failed lookup puts one line under the day's heading, "The exercise videos could not be loaded. The exercises work without them.", with **Try again**.

## Tools videos (`/admin/content/videos`)

`ui/ToolVideos.tsx`, laid out like Component stories: every tool a video can be for (`videoTools`: the plan's tools but `video`, a guitar-play-along once per shape) on the left, each with how many of its levels have a video (`coveredCount`); the picked one (`#<tool key>` in the address) on the right. Its levels, one group per ladder then **No level** (`stepGroups`; a tool with no ladder is the tool alone), each with the video covering it or **Missing**, how many seed exercises use the tool there (`seedToolUse`: every choice of every seed block file, as written, each block once) and **Record** (to `/admin/content/videos/record?tool=<key>&steps=<step>`). Under them every video made for the tool (`made_for` `tool:<key>`), with **Play** (the player loads only then), **Change levels** (re-ticks it with the save's rule: a level another video holds moves to this one, and the line under the heading says which video lost it; none ticked: it shows nowhere), **Edit** (title, note) and **Delete** (only once it covers nothing; the file goes too).

## Recording plan

Which videos to record, in what order, and what is said in each. One video per tool, saved for every level: it shows the tool, plays or sings one example, and ends by inviting the player to try things with it ("Try things with it."). Narrower videos, one for a group of levels, come later and need no change to how videos are stored.

- `model/scripts.ts`: the first 20 videos. `STAGES` is the order, written down as a learning progression (first chords, the two hands, the neck, playing along, the voice, the ears), each stage with its tool keys. `VIDEO_PLANS` holds each one's title (what the video is saved under), about how many minutes it runs, and its `script`: parts of `{ show, say }`, what is on camera and on screen, then the words to say (a new line is a pause). A script is for every level, so it names no key, chord or tune: `[square brackets]` mark what the person recording fills in from the exercise on screen. The words are teaching text (`teaching-voice`), with controls named by their labels on the screen; an icon-only button is "the Play button".
- `model/recordPlan.ts`: `LATER_PLANS`, the tools after the first 20, each with a title and notes and no script yet (listed most used in the seed first), and `NO_VIDEO`, the tools with no video of their own and why (the staff, the backing track and the backing band). Every tool key is in exactly one of the three, and every scripted tool is in one stage (`recordPlan.test.ts`). `recordPlan` puts them together with the seed's use of each tool and how many of its levels have a video.

On Tools videos, **Recording plan** is first on the left and shows when the address names no tool (`#plan`): the stages in order, each video numbered, with its tool, length, seed use, **Recorded**, **N of M levels** or **Missing**, **Record** (the record page with every level ticked) and its **Script**; then **Later** with **Notes**, and **No video planned**. A tool's own page and the record page (for a tool, not for This block only) show the same script or notes (`ui/VideoOutline.tsx`).

## Record (`/admin/content/videos/record`)

The only page that loads the camera and a tool. `?tool=<key>&steps=<step,…>` (from **Record** on Tools videos; the steps in the link come ticked) or `?block=<id>&cell=<n>` (a seed block's tool cell: **This block only** is offered and picked). The page (`src/app/admin/content/videos/record/`) draws the tool with the plan's `ExerciseTool`, with the settings of a seed exercise that uses it (a list when there are several: the first ticked level's first, `seedCells`); this feature never imports plan UI. `ui/RecordVideo.tsx` is the recorder: **Start camera**, the Camera list (kept in this browser), the Input list (`components/RecordInputSelect.tsx`: the app's input, the same choice as the nav's devices menu, with its level and, for an audio interface, its channels; changing it reopens only the mic, and the take records the chosen channel through `openRecordInput`), the Sound list and **Hear the input** (`components/RecordSoundSelect.tsx`: one of the looper's guitar and vocal effect stacks, `INPUT_STACKS` in `instruments/effects/presets.ts`, per input of the device: a mic has one **Sound** list, an audio interface **Input 1 sound** and **Input 2 sound** (only the inputs its channel choice records), so a guitar on Input 1 and a voice on Input 2 each get their own; each input goes through its own `EffectChain` (`openRecordInput`'s `through`, fed by the tap's `parts`) and the take records the two mixed; **No effects** is the input as it comes; an amp is listed only when its NAM model is in this browser's library; the picks are kept in this browser and change the sound without reopening the mic; Hear the input plays them to the speakers, off on every visit), the click (below), **Record** and **Stop** (or R, heard before the page's own keys and marked handled when it acts, so the riff maker's r for a rest skips that press; a second press while the take is still starting is ignored), the tool over the camera (**Tool over the camera** hides it; the take records the camera only), then the take with its title, note and what it shows with: the levels ticked (**All levels**, **None**, one box per level and No level; for a tool with no ladder, the tool) or this block only. Before saving it lists which video would lose which level (`wouldLose`), after saving where players see it and what the save took off other videos. Over the camera the tool sits where **Place** (Bottom, Top, Left, Right, Free), **Size** (Small, Medium, Large) and **Background** (Dark, Clear) put it; with **Free** the tool is moved by the bar on its top and sized by either bottom corner (faint until pointed at), which scales the whole tool and keeps its proportions (`ui/Scaled.tsx`: laid out at `FREE_WIDTH` and scaled to the box's width, over the camera and over the video alike; a narrow tool, the chord shapes (`NARROW_TOOLS`), is laid out at its own width instead, so its chord boxes fill the box; until it is moved the box sits in the top right corner (`DEFAULT_BOX`, or the narrower `NARROW_BOX`); the box is kept as shares of the picture, `Look.box`, its height following the tool; Size is off), and **Tool controls** hides its buttons so only the music shows (the guitar play-along and the melody singer, `CONTROLS_TOOLS`; Space still starts and stops it; for any other tool the box is off). Both are kept in this browser. Tools never scroll themselves here (`NoAutoScroll`): a scroll would move the camera off the screen. The take records the camera only; the tool is never in the file. While a take records, the tool's beat is sampled every 50 ms (the overlay, below), and the take panel says whether the tool will move with the video. The tool is drawn as written (`Tool`, not `ExerciseTool`), so the melody is in the voice it was written with, the one the overlay draws. A failed **Save for players** says why and can be pressed again (only the row is sent when the file is up): the take's video id is given once and stored with the take before the first upload, so a retry, even after a reload, reuses it.

**The click.** In the Sound group, **Click at N bpm** clicks through the speakers while a take records (`client/click.ts` `startClick`, on the AudioContext clock, louder on beat 1; only the input is recorded). A page that gives the tool's `tempo` (`{ bpm, bars, click }`) plays it at that tempo, on by default only when `click` is true: the riff maker's is, the record page's play-along's is not. Without a tempo it is off by default and the tempo is typed. The on/off is kept in this browser per tool (`musickit.content.click.<tool>`) and wins over the page's default; the typed tempo is kept in `musickit.content.click`. While it is on, **Count-in** (0 to 2 bars) and **Length** (bars: the tempo's, else 4) show; the take stops itself half a second after the length. While recording, `ui/BeatView.tsx` shows the count-in counting down, then the bar and beat. Recorded to the click, the take keeps `click` (`{ downbeat, bpm, bars }`: where beat 1 fell in the video, after the output and input latency), and its overlay clock is the click's grid (`model/takeClock.ts` `takeOverlay`), so the tool moves with the video though Play was never pressed on it. Without the click, the tool's own beat is sampled, as below. One clock per take, never both.

**Kept takes.** Each take is kept in this browser (`client/takes.ts`, IndexedDB) under its target (`model/takeKey.ts` `takeKey`: `<block id>:<cell>`, else `tool:<tool>`; ticking levels never swaps it), as soon as it stops, and opens again with the page (`oldTakeKey`: where an older page kept it, a riff's block id; it is kept under the take key from its next change). A take recorded to the click with no overlay (the old riff Take panel saved none) opens with the tool as drawn on the click's grid (`withClickOverlay`). The take's line says **Saved in this browser**, **Saving…** or **Not saved** (with **Save** to try again); **Save file** downloads it (the exported file when there is one) and **Delete take** removes it here. A new recording replaces the kept take; **Back to the previous take** brings back the one it replaced, until the page closes. The browser asks before closing the page only while the take is neither kept here nor saved for players. With Read the neck on, what it reads on the take is kept with it (`TakeLog`) and listed under the take (`ui/DetectLog.tsx`, **Copy log**).

**Face camera** (None by default, kept in this browser) opens a second camera beside the guitar camera. `client/faceCam.ts` draws the guitar camera on a canvas each frame with the face camera's picture as a circle in a corner (**Face corner**, **Face size**, kept in this browser), cut round the face MediaPipe's Face Detector finds a few times a second (`blaze_face_short_range`, fetched the first time); the crop follows the face slowly and holds still through small moves: it zooms only when the face's size (the median of the last second's looks) changes by a fifth, leaning in or back, and then slowly, and moves only when the face moves by 8% of the crop (`steady`); it shows the middle of the face camera until a face is found. The canvas sits over the guitar camera on screen, and the take records the canvas with the input, so both cameras are in the file. A canvas only draws while its tab is in front. A face camera that won't open says so and the take records the guitar camera alone. The circle's place and the crop are pure (`model/faceCircle.ts`).

**A tool's kit.** A page may give `RecordVideo` a `kit` (`ui/kit.ts` `RecordKit`) beside its tool; the riff maker does (`devtools/riff/RiffLab.tsx`: its Sliding tab, Bar by bar and Chord box views and the riff's notes), and the record page does for a guitar play-along (`useToolNotes`: its notes, `loopBeats` and key, with no views; its `tempo` is the tool's bpm and its bars, so the click is on at that tempo), and without one the page is as above. Its `views` add a **View** list to the Tool group (**Tool**, then each view's label; kept per tool in this browser, `musickit.content.view.<tool>`; shown only when the kit has views). With a view picked, the tool's box draws the view instead of the tool: over the camera at the click's beat while recording to it, else the tool's beat, read each animation frame (`LiveView`, which re-renders only when the beat moves by a hundredth); the tool stays mounted, hidden, so its clock and Space still work. Over the take the view sits in the tool's panel (Place, Size and Free apply, `ViewOverTake`) at the take's beat, so Export with overlay records it; the saved overlay is still the tool's own drawing. Its `notes` (one pass, in beats, repeated each `loopBeats`) are timed onto the take's clock (`takeClock`: the overlay's, else the click's grid; neither: no notes) and given to Read the neck, Read the neck (OpenCV) and the detection log. `key` goes to Read the neck (OpenCV) (`NeckCvView`'s `keyOf`, live and over the take) for its **Labels**: Numbers in the key; without one that choice is greyed out. `afterTake(take, video)` draws under the take's Save for players row.

**Read the neck** (off by default) draws the shared neck effects (`src/features/neckFx`) over the camera before and between takes, and over the take: markers, the fret grid, effects and marker-button filters. Not while recording.

The take plays back framed like the camera (16:9, cover), with the tool drawn over it at its clock where the look puts it (`ToolOverVideo`, through the page's `OverlayDrawProvider`) and, with Read the neck on, the neck effects. **Export with overlay** (below) burns what is over it into a new file; **Save for players** then uploads that file with no `overlay` (the tool is in the picture, so it isn't drawn twice), and **Use the take without overlay** goes back to the plain take and its overlay.

## Export with overlay

`client/burn.ts` `burnOverlay(box, video, seconds)`: the take is played once from its start, in real time, and the box holding it (the video and everything drawn over it) is recorded with Chrome's Element Capture (`getDisplayMedia` with `preferCurrentTab`, then `track.restrictTo(RestrictionTarget.fromElement(box))`; Region Capture's `cropTo` where that is missing) plus the take's own sound (`video.captureStream()`). The file is the box's size on screen in device pixels. The video's controls are hidden while it plays, and NeckView's status line too (`bare`). The browser asks which tab to share: it must be this one, and the tab stays in front until the take ends (a hidden tab stops drawing). **Stop export** or Stop sharing ends it with nothing kept. `canBurn()` says whether the browser can (Chrome 104+, Element Capture 132+); without it the button isn't offered. Used by the recorder (`ui/RecordVideo.tsx`, on the record page and the riff maker), which keeps the exported file with the take in IndexedDB (`burned`; an old riff take's `withOverlay` is read as it), and Save file and Save for players use it. The box needs `isolate` (a stacking context) for Element Capture.

## The overlay

A tool drawn over its video while the video plays, in step with the hands, with no sound and no mic. Today the guitar play-along (every shape) and the melody singer (`OVERLAY_TOOLS` in `model/overlay.ts`); any other tool's video just plays.

- **Recording** (without the click; to the click the clock is the click's grid, above): the tool reports its beat with `useToolClock` (`components/practice/toolClock.tsx`): the play-along its beat counting on through its loops, the singer the beat in the tune (a loop jumps back); null while stopped, held while paused. `RecordVideo` listens (`ToolClockListener`), samples it every 50 ms from the recording's start, and on Stop squeezes the samples into straight pieces (`model/clock.ts` `squeeze`: `[t, beat, rate]`, within 0.06 beats of every sample; a tempo change, a loop, a start or a stop starts a new piece). A take whose tool was never started saves no overlay.
- **Saved** in `content_videos.overlay` as `{ tool, clock, look }` (`model/overlay.ts`): the look is where the tool sat when the take was saved (`readLook`, a part that doesn't read is the default); a copy of the tool's settings, so a later edit of the exercise doesn't move the overlay off the hands. The save reads it again (`readOverlay`: the plan's own parser, stored mode) and refuses one that doesn't read or isn't for the target's tool.
- **Playback**: `ui/OverlayVideo.tsx` plays the video and, when it has an overlay and the page gives a way to draw it (`OverlayDrawProvider` with the plan's `drawToolOverlay`), draws the tool where its look puts it (`ui/lookPanel.ts` `panelClass` and `panelStyle`, kept clear of the video's own controls but for a Free one, which sits where it was put; a video saved with no look: at the top, medium, dark; a placed panel also sets `--kit-max-h`, the most a tall kit view may stand in it, in `cqh` of the recorder's camera frame and take box, which are size containers) at `beatAt(clock, video.currentTime)`: it moves while the video plays, holds while it is paused and follows a seek exactly. **Tool over the video** hides it. The tool's drawing is its `VideoOverlay` (`exercises/play-along/PlayAlong.tsx`, `exercises/basics/melody-singer`), picked by `plan/ui/ToolOverlay.tsx`: the same tab, notes, neck or staff as the tool, with no buttons and no sound; the player's own settings for the tool are not touched. Used by Watch video on a plan card, the library strip and Play on Tools videos.

## The library strip

An exercise opened on `/admin/content/library` (its row, `LibraryItem` → `ExamplePreview` with `videos`) is drawn inside `ui/LibraryStrip.tsx`: the card reports its tools as drawn (`onAsks`, so a chip that changes a level changes the strip) and the strip, through the card's `afterTool` (`BlockBody`'s slot under each tool), puts one line under each tool: the video players see there and where it comes from (**For this block only**, or **For picking-drill, beginner and intermediate** from the list's steps), **Play** (the player loads then), and **Record** (a seed block's tool: the record page for that block and cell; a block with no seed file: for the tool at that cell's level). A tool whose video showed higher up in the block says "Same video as a tool above", one with none "No video for … at …". One lookup per drawing; a failed one says so on each line, with **Try again**. Nothing records here.

## The seed and `blocks.videos`

`db:seed-blocks` (`upsertCurated` in `userData/server`) never writes `blocks.videos` in its insert or update. The one write is a drop: when a block's tool cells moved (`model/keptCells.ts`: a changed count of tool cells drops every entry; another tool key at an index drops that entry), the moved entries are removed and printed (`video dropped  <block>  cell N  <video>  (the video is kept; point the cell at it again)`, and `N cell videos dropped` in the summary). The video's row and file stay. Text edits and a changed level keep the entry.

## Routes (`src/app/api/content-videos`)

| Route | Who | What |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `upload` POST | `content` and signed in | The client-upload token. `clientPayload` `{ id, type, size, madeFor }`; only `content/videos/<madeFor>/<id>.<ext>`, WebM or MP4 up to 1 GB, never over an existing file; a taken id is 409. 503 without a Blob token. |
| `/` GET | `content` | Every video, newest first: `{ videos }`. |
| `/` POST | `content` and signed in | After the upload: `{ id, title, note, type, seconds, width, height, path, madeFor, target, overlay? }` (an overlay that doesn't read or isn't for the target's tool is 400). The file must be in the store and private (a public one is deleted and refused); its size is the store's. Saves the row: `{ video, lost }`. 409 for a taken id, a block not in the database, a cell past the block's tool cells, or a cell whose stored tool is not the target's `tool`. |
| `[id]` PATCH | `content` | `{ title?, note? }`; one left out stays as it is. |
| `[id]/steps` PUT | `content` | **Change levels**: `{ steps }` (the save's rule; `[]` takes them all off, `null` is the whole tool for a tool with no ladder): `{ video, lost }`. A block's own video is 409. |
| `[id]` DELETE | `content` | Refused (409 with `pointers`) while a tool step or a block's cell points at the video; else the row, then the file (a file that can't be deleted is logged, the answer stays 200). |
| `[id]/file` GET | anyone | The file with Range, no session, no cookies, cached `public, max-age=3600` (a deleted video may play from a cache for up to an hour). A bad or unknown id, or a site with no database or store, is a 404. |
| `lookup` POST | anyone | `{ blocks: [{ key, blockId?, cells: [{ cell, tool, step }] }] }` (at most 60 blocks) answers `{ blocks: { [key]: [{ cell, id, from, repeat }] }, videos: { [id]: PlayInfo } }`, never cached. No database: empty. 400 for a bad body; 502 (logged) when the repo throws. |

Signed out with the `content` permission (development opens it to everyone), a save answers 401 "Log in to save a video.": `recorded_by` needs an account. A request that isn't a GET and doesn't come from this site is treated as signed out (`permissionCheck`), so in production it is a 404.
