# Learning components: rules

Layout and layers: `README.md`.

- Layers 0 and 2 (`timeline/`, `shapes/`, `cues/`) stay pure: no React, no sound, no `localStorage`. They depend only downward.
- The AI never writes events, frets, strings, derived note names or seconds; components derive them.
- **Add a basic:** `basics/<name>/index.tsx` (View) and `<name>.props.ts` with a `@component` JSDoc and documented props; run `npm run docs:exercises`; add its parse to `TOOLS` in `src/features/plan/model/tools.ts`; add a view in the plan's `ui/Tool.tsx` (the switch on `tool.type`); a tour in `guide/toolTours.ts` (its test wants one per tool); test; check it shows on `/admin/content/stories` (`devtools/stories/stories.ts` lists every tool in `TOOLS` by itself, and its test fails when one has no story, no docs or props that do not parse). Add hand-written stories there for corners the examples miss.
- **Add a shape:** a file in `shapes/` expanding to a timeline, registered in `shapes/index.ts`, documented in `shapes.props.ts`; regenerate docs; parse in the plan registry; test in `shapes.test.ts`.
- The `interval` shape works out no fret: its pairs are `intervalWalk` and `intervalPairs` (`lib/chordKit/scaleChords.ts`), and a step carries only its pitch and its string. A new way to walk an interval is new ordering in `shapes/interval.ts`, never fret arithmetic there or in a view. It is a shape of `guitar-play-along`, not a tool of its own (user, 2026-11-22: intervals lead into riffs and tunes on one tool).
- The `riff` shape is the one place frets and strings are written down, and a person writes them, in the riff maker (`/admin/content/riff`), into the seed block that holds the riff. The AI is never offered the shape (`PROMPT_HIDDEN_SHAPES` in `plan/model/tools.ts`), so its doc changes no prompt; a block that plays a riff comes from the library. A riff has no articulation yet (no bend, slide, hammer-on or muted stroke): add one to `RiffNote`, `readRiff` and `writeRiff` together, never in a view.
- **Theory tools** (`relative-modes`, `mode-intervals`, `key-chords`, `triads`, `progressions`, `circle-of-fifths`, `piano-keys`, `improvisation`, `tunings`, `chord-circle`, `caged-neck`) take a `key` and a `scale` and draw a creator plugin or a chordKit view in that key (`key-panel/KeyPanel.tsx`, `keyChoiceFor`). They may import the creator's `index.ts`, nothing deeper. A key the panel changes from inside holds for that view only and never writes the tool. `double-stops-neck` takes a `key` and a `scale` too, and draws the double stops page's neck (`components/chordKit/DoubleStopsExplorer`) with no settings: every fret comes from `dyadWalk` (`lib/chordKit/scaleChords.ts`), none is worked out in the view.
- `song-chart` plays through the backing band, never a band of its own: `Band`, `useBand` and `BandMixer` from `@/features/backing`, numerals from `chartNumerals` (`song-chart/chart.ts`). It never drops a bar: a chord the band cannot voice goes to the band as a rest and is named on the chart.
- `staff-reading` draws on `SingingStaff`, never a staff of its own, and takes where a note sits from the staff's `place` (`staff-reading/reading.ts`). It keeps no score: every answer, right or wrong, says the note and moves on.
- `vowel-guide` hears a pitch, never a vowel: no word on the tool, in its props or in a block says the app checks the vowel. Its notes come from the range code the other voice tools use (`useVoiceRange`, `voiceMapIn`, the hill's `zoneFor`) and stay `UNDER_TOP` under the top of the range (`vowel-guide/vowels.ts`); a new or beginner level never climbs. Its mouth is drawn in code from three numbers: no photo, no face, no character. What it says about the voice stays plain and widely taught, with no medical or anatomical claim.
- **A tool drawn over a content video** (today `guitar-play-along` and `melody-singer`) reports its beat with `useToolClock` (`components/practice/toolClock.tsx`) and exports a `VideoOverlay` that draws the tool at a given beat with no buttons, no sound and no mic, from the same drawing code as the tool. Its view and its overlay share that code; never a second drawing. See `contentVideos/README.md` (The overlay).
- Never hand-edit `docs/docs.generated.json`; the freshness test in `docs/extract.test.ts` fails when it is stale.
- Embedded components never write the address bar.
- Every change: tests (cached tsx), `tsc --noEmit`, eslint on this feature.
