# Instruments: rules

Instruments shared by the looper and the pages: the Drone and the step sequencers (drum machine, bass synth). Layout in `index.ts`.

- **One sound, many hosts.** Sound and maths (`drone/drone.ts`, `sequencer/*.ts`, `jam/clock.ts`, `jam/rig.ts`) have no React and are re-exported by `model.ts`, the only entry the looper engine (`src/lib/looper`) may import. React pieces come from `index.ts`.
- **Controls own no audio and have no play button.** `DroneControls`, `PatternBar`, `StepGrid` and `PartLevel` (a part's mute and volume, the same control in the looper's sequencer window, the jam and the pocket tools; its `MuteButton` alone is the mute on every line of the pocket tools and the jam's drone card) take values and callbacks; the host adds its own parts through slots (`header`, `leading`, `trailing`). The looper starts and stops from its stage circle on the beat; a page (`Jam`) has its own play buttons. Layout follows the space given (container queries), so the same controls fit a page and a looper window.
- **One rig hook**: `jam/useJamRig.ts` is the core both page hosts share (`Jam` and `PocketTools`): the `JamRig` and its snapshot, the host's save read back after hydration and written on every change, `ready` (audio on a click), `start` and `toggle` on the next beat. A new host of the jam's parts uses it rather than its own rig wiring.
- **Page host**: `Jam` (`/theory/metronome-drone`) is drone, drums and bass on one clock (`JamRig`, read through `useSyncExternalStore`); parts start and stop on the next beat; one big Play all / Stop all (Stop all while any part plays; Space toggles it outside text fields); the tempo slider sits in the metronome card; a tempo change restarts the playing parts together. The bass plays a minor pentatonic in the drone's key (`Sequencer.key`, `bassRootMidi`: every key between E1 and D♯2). The drums and bass each have a mute and volume (`PartLevel`, `JamRig.setLevel` on the part's `toSpeakers`, saved as `levels`). A **metronome** card comes first (`new JamRig({ followClick: true })`): the click runs whenever any part or loop plays (it joins on the next beat and stops with them), muted by default (`setClickMuted`, saved as `click.muted`); its own play button starts it alone and unmutes it, and Stop all stops it too. The pocket tools' rig has no `followClick`: its click plays only from its own button. Saved in localStorage `musickit.jam` (the old `musickit.drone` is read when there is no jam save). Under the parts are its **loops** (`jam/loops.ts`, maths in `jam/take.ts`, UI `JamLoops.tsx`): each records 1, 2, 4 or 8 bars from its own input (a device and channel 1, 2 or both mixed; one shared stream per device) from the next bar line, or after a one-bar count-in when nothing plays, then plays round on the jam's clock; each loop's input has an effect chain (`JamEffects.tsx`, the shared effects of `effects/`, NAM included once the page renders `WireNam`) that is in the take, and a headphones toggle (`monitor`) to hear the effected input live (off by default; switching it on opens the input); Play all starts and stops them too and the tempo is locked while a loop holds sound. A device opens only on a click (record or Connect). Loop settings are saved in `musickit.jam` (`loops`), never the sound. Up to 8 loops (+ under them).
- **Pocket tools**: `PocketTools` (`pocket/`) is a small play-along band for a page's settings bar, hosted in the scales settings (`ScaleSettingsBar`, on unless its `config` has `pocket: false`, which also carries `clickAlways` and `onStatus`: under the settings, on the right of a top bar, or stacked one tool per line in the side column with `stacked`): a tall Play all, then one line each for the click, drone, drums and bass, every part on its own `JamRig` (the same clock as `Jam`; the rig also has the metronome click, `setClick`, from `click/click.ts`'s `ClickTrack` on the rig's grid). Each line keeps the controls you reach for and a sliders button with the rest (full `DroneControls`; `PatternBar` + `StepGrid`). The drone, and so the bass, follows the host's `keyPc`. Space stops whatever plays and the next Space brings it back; otherwise Space is left to the page. Every line has a mute (`MuteButton`): the click's is `setClickMuted` (a muted click still keeps the grid), the drone's, drums' and bass's are `setLevel` (a muted part keeps playing, unheard). Saved in localStorage `musickit.pocket` (no loops; nothing plays on load, except that with `clickAlways` the click starts muted on the first press anywhere on the page, since audio can't start before one: the singing page keeps its melody bar on it). `onStatus` hands the host a `PocketStatus` (tempo, drone note, what plays) for its header, plus `nextBeat`, `gridBeats` (beats since the grid's beat 1, `JamRig.gridBeats`) and `restart` (the grid over on beat 1, or `from` beats into it so a host can start part-way with the click still on its beats, `JamRig.restart`, the click joining muted) for a host that plays on the click, and `heard`/`hear` (the click heard or muted, started if need be) and `setTempo` for a host's own metronome buttons. The click's line is two lines: its buttons, then a tempo slider.
- **Effects** (`effects/`): the effects of the looper and the jam (`EFFECT_DEFS`, `createEffect`, `EffectChain`, choice sources, `EffectControls`), moved here from the looper so both can use them; exported from `model.ts`. The NAM amp is injected with `setNamFactory` and `registerChoice` (`src/components/namWiring.ts`); never import `@/features/nam` here.
- Imports only `@/features/sound` and shared components (`Icon`, the design system `@/components/ui`). Never the looper, chordKit or the creator.
- New instrument: sound in a no-React file with tests for its maths, exported from `model.ts`; controls that take values and callbacks, exported from `index.ts`; then host it in the looper (an action, saved state) and/or a page.
- Run the tests (`npx tsx --test src/features/instruments/*/*.test.ts src/lib/looper/*.test.ts`), `npx tsc --noEmit` and lint before every commit.
