# Instruments: rules

Instruments shared by the looper and the pages: the Drone and the step sequencers (drum machine, bass synth). Layout in `index.ts`.

- **One sound, many hosts.** Sound and maths (`drone/drone.ts`, `sequencer/*.ts`, `jam/clock.ts`, `jam/rig.ts`, `loops/deck.ts`, `loops/take.ts`) have no React and are re-exported by `model.ts`, the only entry the looper engine (`src/lib/looper`) may import. React pieces come from `index.ts`.
- **Controls own no audio and have no play button.** `DroneControls`, `PatternBar`, `StepGrid` and `PartLevel` (a part's mute and volume, the same control in the looper's sequencer window and the band; its `MuteButton` alone is the mute on every line of the `pocket` view and the `full` view's drone card) take values and callbacks; the host adds its own parts through slots (`header`, `leading`, `trailing`). The looper starts and stops from its stage circle on the beat; a page (the `full` PracticeBand) has its own play buttons. Layout follows the space given (container queries), so the same controls fit a page and a looper window.
- **One rig hook**: `jam/useJamRig.ts` is the core every page host shares (`PracticeBand` and `JamPage`): the `JamRig` and its snapshot, the host's save read back after hydration and written on every change, `ready` (audio on a click), `start` and `toggle` on the next beat. A new host of the jam's parts uses it rather than its own rig wiring.
- **Practice band**: `PracticeBand` (`band/PracticeBand.tsx`) is the one play-along band: click, drone, drums and bass on one `JamRig` clock (the click is `setClick`, `click/click.ts`'s `ClickTrack` on the rig's grid), drawn by a `view` (`band/views/`) and set up by a `config` (`band/config.ts`: `parts`, `bpm`, `keyPc`, `groove`, `droneOctave`, and `show` flags over the view's row in `VIEW_SHOW`: part chips, tempo, tempo slider, beats, key, levels, groove, patterns, drone sound, More). Parts start and stop on the next beat; the bass plays a minor pentatonic in the drone's key (`Sequencer.key`, `bassRootMidi`: every key between E1 and D♯2; its lanes are degrees, so a key change keeps the rhythm). It makes its own rig from `storageKey`, or plays on a host's `rig` (a `useJamRig` result) and then neither restores nor saves. `restoreBand` loads the save and, in `bar` and `inline`, puts the host's bpm, key and groove over it on every mount (saved part levels stay behind there, so every part is heard); in `full` and `pocket` the save wins (`pocket` also reads the first pocket saves, only a drone and a click). **Grooves**: `GrooveSelect` picks one of `GROOVES` (`sequencer/grooves.ts`, one 4/4 bar of drums and bass roles plus its `swing`, shared with the backing track) for the drums and bass (`JamRig.setGroove`, saved as `groove`); editing a step or pattern turns it to "Custom" (`groove` null). Swing moves the off-beat eighths late (0 straight, as before; 1 triplet). 4/4 only (`BEATS_PER_BAR`). Saves per host: `musickit.jam` (the jam page; the old `musickit.drone` is read when there is no jam save), `musickit.pocket` (the pocket view on the scales pages and the stage), `musickit.practiceBand` (the bar and inline views).
  - `full` (`band/views/FullView.tsx`, cards in `PartCard.tsx`): the jam page's cards, one per part in `config.parts`, a metronome card first, one big Play all / Stop all (Space toggles it outside text fields; it uses the host's `ready`), the tempo slider in the metronome card, the groove select in the drums card's pattern bar, a mute and volume per part (`PartLevel`, `JamRig.setLevel`, saved as `levels`). The jam page's rig has `followClick`: the click runs, muted by default (`setClickMuted`, saved as `click.muted`), whenever a part or loop plays; its own play button starts it alone and unmutes it.
  - `pocket` (`band/views/PocketView.tsx`, its shared pieces in `pocketParts.tsx`): the band in a page's settings bar, hosted by the scales settings (`ScaleSettingsBar`, on unless its `config` has `pocket: false`, which also carries `clickAlways`, `onStatus` and `drone: false`, the last leaving `drone` out of `config.parts`) and the stage's band widget. A tall Play all (the config's parts), then one line per part: each keeps the controls you reach for and a sliders button with the rest (full `DroneControls`; the drums' groove, `PatternBar` and `StepGrid`). The click's line is two lines: its buttons, then a labelled tempo slider. Host props go in `pocket`: `stacked` (one tool per line in the side column), `className`, `lines` (the host's own lines under the drone, each a `PocketLine` with `POCKET_BUTTON`/`POCKET_LIT` buttons: the scales settings' **Input** line with `config.input`, the input chip as the mic button; `ScaleSettings.tsx` owns the listener, so nothing here imports the mic or chordKit, and `WithSettings` lights the heard note on the necks, `HeardNote`; the singing page leaves it off, it has its own mic). The drone, and so the bass, follows `config.keyPc` live. Space stops whatever plays and the next Space brings it back; otherwise Space is left to the page. Every line has a mute (`MuteButton`): the click's is `setClickMuted` (a muted click still keeps the grid), the parts' are `setLevel` (a muted part keeps playing, unheard). Its rig has no `followClick`: the click plays only from its own button. Nothing plays on load, except that with `clickAlways` the click starts muted on the first press anywhere on the page (audio can't start before one: the singing page keeps its melody bar on it). `onStatus` hands the host a `PocketStatus` (tempo, drone note, what plays) for its header, plus `nextBeat`, `gridBeats` (beats since the grid's beat 1, `JamRig.gridBeats`) and `restart` (the grid over on beat 1, or `from` beats into it, `JamRig.restart`, the click joining muted) for a host that plays on the click, and `heard`/`hear` and `setTempo` for a host's own metronome buttons.
  - `bar` (plan exercises: backing-band): one line: a Play that starts only the chosen parts, a chip per offered part (joins or leaves on the next beat while playing; `config.start` sets which are lit when it opens, `config.locked` the ones that cannot be switched off), tempo −/+, beat dots and the key; "More" unfolds a tempo slider, the drone octave, the groove and the drum and bass patterns (`show: { more: false }` hides it: the plan passes its detail level; hidden, the drone octave and the patterns play at their defaults under the host's groove, while the saved values stay and return with "More"). It takes a step's audio focus group from `useStepPart` (`@/components/practice/exerciseStep`), so a step's "Start exercise" plays it with the rest of the step.
  - `inline` (the staff): the same parts with no part chips and no play button: it plays while its `on` prop is set, shows what plays, mutes and the beat dots, and calls `onStop` when another source takes the audio focus or it scrolls away; the staff hands it its `group` and has its own Play beside the mic.
- **Loops**: `JamPage` (`jam/JamPage.tsx`, `/theory/metronome-drone`) owns the rig (`useJamRig` with `followClick`) and its loops (`useLoopDeck`), and draws `<PracticeBand view="full" rig={…} ready={…}>` then the loops card. The loops are a `LoopDeck` (`loops/deck.ts`, its loops in `loops/loops.ts`, maths in `loops/take.ts`, UI `Loops` in `loops/LoopList.tsx`), the looper on its own, which follows the rig as a `GridFollower` (`rig.follow(deck)`, made by `useLoopDeck(rig)`): the rig tells it the grid and the tempo, starts and stops it with the parts when asked (`withFollowers`; Play all and Stop all, `snapshot().followersOn`), and asks it whether it keeps the grid (and so the following click) and whether it locks the tempo (while a loop holds sound); the rig itself has no loops. Each loop records 1, 2, 4 or 8 bars from its input: by default the app's one input (`deviceId` "", both channels mixed; handed to the deck's `attach` by `useLoopDeck` as an `AppInput` so `loops.ts` stays free of app state, followed when it changes, its levels published for the input chip, which the loop shows in place of the channel buttons), or a device of its own picked as an override (channel 1, 2 or both mixed; saved loops that name a device keep it); one shared stream per device from the next bar line, or after a one-bar count-in when nothing plays, then plays round on the jam's clock. Each loop's input has an effect chain (`loops/JamEffects.tsx`, the shared effects of `effects/`, NAM included once the page renders `WireNam`) that is in the take, and a headphones toggle (`monitor`) to hear the effected input live (off by default; switching it on opens the input). A device opens only on a click (record or Connect). Loop settings are saved in `musickit.jam` (`loops`, written by the page beside the rig's save: `jam/page.ts`'s `saveJamPage`/`loadJamPage` and `JamPageSave`; `JamSaved` knows nothing of loops), never the sound. Up to 8 loops (+ under them).
- **Effects** (`effects/`): the effects of the looper and the jam (`EFFECT_DEFS`, `createEffect`, `EffectChain`, choice sources, `EffectControls`), moved here from the looper so both can use them; exported from `model.ts`. The NAM amp is injected with `setNamFactory` and `registerChoice` (`src/components/namWiring.ts`); never import `@/features/nam` here.
- Imports only `@/features/sound` and shared components (`Icon`, `InputChip`, the design system `@/components/ui`). Never the looper, chordKit or the creator.
- New instrument: sound in a no-React file with tests for its maths, exported from `model.ts`; controls that take values and callbacks, exported from `index.ts`; then host it in the looper (an action, saved state) and/or a page.
- Run the tests (`npx tsx --test src/features/instruments/*/*.test.ts src/lib/looper/*.test.ts`), `npx tsc --noEmit` and lint before every commit.
