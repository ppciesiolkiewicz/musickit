# Students

A teacher adds a student by name, email and the answers to the first questionnaire. That makes a normal account for the
student (role student, on the `taught` plan, so no setup), a `teacher_students` link, and a plan document that holds the
answers. The student signs in the first time with a claim link, mailed by the teacher's route.

## Parts

- `server/students.ts`: the rules. `addStudent` (checks in a fixed order: role, name, email, answers, a place this month (`placesOf`, `canGive`), an email that already has an account: `listed` when it is this teacher's student, `taught` when another teacher's (a student has one teacher for now; the line points to Send feedback), else `exists`; a step that fails after the account is made deletes the account again; a failing delete is logged and the first error is the one thrown), `listStudents` (newest first, with the plan's limit, its extras and the active count on the teacher's `today`), `placesOf` (the limit, the extras and the students active in `today`'s month, from `teacher_students.planned_on`), `newClaimLink` (the teacher's own unclaimed student, one a minute, at most `CLAIM_DAY_LIMIT` (5, the first one counted) in a day: `daily`, 429), `peekClaim` and `openClaim` (the claim token is used once, signs in, records `claimedAt`, goes to `/welcome`; any other sign-in of the student records it too, `startSession` in the account feature), `CLAIM_TTL` (30 days).
- `server/plans.ts`: a teacher's student's weeks. `studentView` (the student's row, the current week the teacher made, `teacherWeekOn(today)` else the newest started, with the teacher's comment, and a slider per topic of the student's areas: `now`, the `outcome()` score today, 0 when nothing is known; `atStart`, the score the day before that week started, null with no week; `upcoming`, the start of the week made that hasn't started, `upcomingTeacherWeek`, else null). `makeWeek` (the link checked first; then a place this month: a student not yet active is refused with `limit` when the limit and extras are used, one already active always gets a week; then from the document read with its version: the moved sliders recorded with `setScores` against `now`, on the teacher's day (`today`, so an evening west of UTC counts that evening) (topics of the student's areas, finite, clamped 0..3; a value sent unchanged records nothing), the comment (trimmed, at most 2000 characters, empty changes nothing) on the current week, never the new one, the start (rotate: the running week's start, replacing it; next: the upcoming week's start, replacing it, so there is at most one upcoming week (dropped when untouched, else to the history), else the day after the running week's last day; no running week: today), seven days composed (`composeStudentWeek`, seed `studentId:start:madeAt`, paced as of today at the earliest, the last day of the week before barred on day one) and put first in `given` (`giveWeek`), fitted to the cap (`fitPlanState`; still too big: `too-big`, nothing written), written with the version read, then the student marked active (`markPlanned`, the teacher's `today`; a failed mark is logged, the week stays); the answer's `comment` is `stored`, `none` or `no-week`; a stale write starts again from a fresh read, three tries, then `busy`; `no-answers` and `no-blocks` write nothing at all).
- `server/mail.ts`: `claimEmail` and `claimUrl` (`/claim?token=`). Sent with `sendMail` from `features/account/server/mail.ts`.
- `server/http.ts`: the routes as functions over explicit deps (`StudentsHttpDeps`) and a `Request`; `server/deps.ts` builds the real ones; the `route.ts` files under `src/app/api/students` and `src/app/api/auth/claim` only call them. Every error body is `{ error, code }`; the sign-in answers (401 `logged-out`, 409 `account-changed`) carry the lines in `model/words.ts`, which the page shows for the same codes. `busy` names the button pressed (Make next week's plan, or Rotate this week's plan). `POST /api/students` adds, makes the first week, mails the link, and keeps the student whatever happens after the add (`plan`: made, short, none; `mail`: sent, off, failed). A student id that is not a uuid is a 404 before any query. `today` is checked with `isNearToday` from `plan/server/composeToday.ts`.
- `model/places.ts`: active students, pure. A teacher's plan limits the students given a week in a calendar month (added with a first week, Make next week's plan, Rotate this week's plan), not the list; `extraStudents` more a month are free (Solo 2, Music school 3). `activeOn`, `monthStart`, `placesState` (`open`, `room`, `at-limit`, `extras`, `full`), `canGive`, and the words: `countLine` ("12 of 15 active students"), `COUNT_HELP` (the (i) beside it), `placesLine` (the notice from the limit on) and `fullLine` (also the routes' `limit` answer, 409, naming the plan's numbers).
- `model/words.ts`: the sign-in lines both sides use (`LOGGED_OUT_WORDS`, `ACCOUNT_CHANGED_WORDS` from the account feature).
- `model/types.ts`: the shapes the routes send (`StudentRow`, `StudentList`, `AddedStudent`, `TopicSlider`, `StudentView`, `MadePlan`), shared by `server/` and the page, so no client file imports `server/`.
- `model/sliders.ts`: the sliders, pure. `LEVEL_MARKS` (New, Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced), `STEP` (0.1), `tenth`, `levelWord` ("Beginner, 40% to Intermediate"), `moved(start, now)` (only the topics moved by half a step or more: a topic sent is recorded as the teacher's score and holds from then on, so an untouched slider is never sent).
- `client.ts`: the pages' calls, `fetchStudents`, `postStudent`, `postClaimLink`, `fetchStudent` (one student's view; an answer with no `upcoming` reads as null), `postStudentPlan` (make a week), and the claim page's `fetchClaim` (whose link it is, `GET`, spends nothing) and `postClaim` (spends it; `next` only a path on this site, else `/welcome`). None throws; each answers `{ ok: false, code, error }` with the route's own code and words, or the page's (`offline`, `failed`, `logged-out`, `account-changed`, `CLIENT_WORDS`). `account-changed` is known by its `code` (or by the bare `error` other data routes send). 20 s timeout, `accountHeaders`.
- The page, `/students` (`src/app/students/page.tsx`, `ui/StudentsApp.tsx`): `StudentsView` draws every state from props (who is looking, the list loading / failed with Try again / empty / listed, the count as "n of m active students" with an (i) saying what counts (`InfoTip`, hover or tap), from the limit on a notice (`placesLine`) with Change your plan (`/account`) and Send feedback (`/feedback`), Add a student disabled only when the limit and extras are used, the line after an add, in the warning tone when the plan or the email didn't go as meant (`addedLook`), each row's Send the link again). A row: the name, a link to `/students/<id>`; the email; "Not opened yet" or the date the link was first opened; Send the link again until it is opened. With no account (accounts off, or signed out) or an account that can't have students, a line says why, never a blank page.
- `ui/AddStudent.tsx`: adding, one step modal. Name and email first (both checked before Next), then the player's questionnaire worded for a student (`questionSteps(…, { forStudent: true })`), then a screen saying what Add student does. `today` is the device's date (`localDate()`). A failed add keeps everything typed: `name`, `email` and `exists` go back to the first screen with the line under the field, any other error shows on the last screen (`afterAdd` in `model/addFlow.ts`, pure and tested); any edit clears the line; it closes only with Close or after the add (`addedLine`: what happened to the plan, `made` / `short` / `none`, and the email, `sent` / `off` / `failed`). The flow survives a reload (sessionStorage, `ADD_KEY`).
- A student's page, `/students/<id>` (`src/app/students/[id]/page.tsx`, `ui/StudentPage.tsx`): `StudentPageView` draws every state from props (who is looking, as on `/students` with Log in coming back here; loading; a 404 as the not-found state with a link to Students; another failure with Try again; the student). The student: name and email; under them the line that a next plan is made and when it starts, when one is (`waitingLine`, by the same rule as `upcomingLine` below), and a link, Next plan, down to that part (`#next-plan`: on a phone the week between is very long); the current week read-only as the student sees it (`GivenPlanView` with no `onRate`, `onTime` or `onEdit`: no stars to press, no day timer, no Edit; with `ratedBy` the student's name, so a rated exercise shows their stars read-only under "How it went for Ben" (`ExerciseCard`, `StarRating` without `onChange`); the days and the tools still open), headed "This week" while it runs and "Last plan" after, or "No plan yet"; then "Next plan": `ui/TopicSliders.tsx` (the `Slider` atom, named by the topic, in the area's hue, grouped by area with `AREA_LOOK`, 0 to 3 step 0.1, opening at `now`, a mark at `atStart`, the four levels under the scale, the value in words beside it), "Anything else about this week?" (only with a week; it opens with the comment already saved on that week, and an unchanged comment is not sent), the line that a next plan is made, when one is (`upcomingLine`: its start, and that Make next week's plan makes a new one in its place; not drawn right after a next week is made, when `madeLine` above it says the same), Make next week's plan, and Rotate this week's plan while the week runs, which asks once in a `Modal` ("Rotate this week's plan?", Rotate the plan / Keep this plan) before replacing it. Only moved sliders are sent (`planInput`); after a plan is made (`afterMake`) the page loads the student again (`viewOf`: the sliders open at the new scores, the comment box at the comment saved on the week now shown) and `madeLine` says what was made and from which date (`dayWords`' date title, "Monday 23 November"), a short week, and a comment that had no week to go on. A failed make shows the route's line (for `limit`, with Change your plan and Send feedback, `ui/PlanWays.tsx`, as on `/students`) and keeps the sliders and the comment where the teacher left them. Moving a slider again clears the line about the plan made. These rules are pure in `model/makeFlow.ts`.
- The student's side. `/claim?token=` (`src/app/claim/page.tsx`, `ui/ClaimLink.tsx`, public in `src/lib/publicPaths.ts`): `ClaimLinkView` draws every state from props. The page's GET only reads whose account it is ("This is Ben's attunr account. Your teacher made it for you."); **Log in as Ben** posts the token and loads `next` with `window.location.replace` (a full load: the stores are per session). The button waits for the session read, so a person logged in to another account in that browser is told whose account they are in before they press. A dead link (410) shows the route's `gone` line and Log in (to `/login?next=/plan`: their email already has the account), nothing else to press; a failed check shows its line with Try again. `/welcome` (`src/app/welcome/page.tsx`, `ui/Welcome.tsx`, a stub): "Hello, Ben", one line (that their teacher made them a plan, or, with no week made yet, that it shows on the practice plan page once their teacher makes it), **Open your practice plan** (`/plan`); an account that isn't `taught` (or accounts off) is sent to `/plan`, a logged-out visitor to log in by the proxy. Their plan page is the plan feature's (`/plan` for `Account.taught`: the teacher's weeks, never a composed day).
- The teacher's comment stays on the server: `GET /api/me/documents/plan` answers without it (`readOwnDocVersioned`) and the student's save keeps the stored teacher records (`writeDoc(..., { owner: true })`, `features/userData/server/data.ts`); a share of a week carries only its days.
- `ui/TeacherOnly.tsx`: the nav's Students link (`SiteNav`) shows only for a signed-in account with `can("students")`. On a phone it is its icon; under 380 px Interactive notes is its icon too (`nav-fold` in `globals.css`; under 340 px for every account), so the links fit a 320 px screen.
- The limit is the teacher's own plan row: `students` null is no limit (and no extras); no plan row, or 0, is no places. It counts active students this month, `model/places.ts`; a student added before `planned_on` existed counts from the next week made.
- Tests: `server/*.test.ts` over `memoryRepo` and `memoryUserDataRepo`; `plans.test.ts` with the plan feature's `stubLibrary`; `client.test.ts` over a fake `fetch`; `model/addFlow.test.ts`; `ui/ClaimLink.test.tsx` (the claim page's states and the welcome page); `ui/StudentsApp.test.tsx` and `ui/StudentPage.test.tsx` draw the pages' states with `renderToStaticMarkup`; `model/sliders.test.ts`; `model/places.test.ts`; `afterMake`, `planInput` and `viewOf` in `ui/StudentPage.test.tsx`.
