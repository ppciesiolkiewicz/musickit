D1. One "Start exercise" button for drone and mic

Problem: Singing exercises tell the student to press Play on the drone and then start the mic on the staff. That is two controls and two sentences in every exercise.
Proposal: One button starts the backing (drone or click) and the mic together. This would be a combined control in the tool tree, such as a staff option that takes its own drone and click, or a wrapper tool.
Acceptance: The text says "Press Start below". Stopping stops both. An exercise can still use a drone without the mic.
Open question: Does this replace the click-drone plus staff pair, or is it a new tool the prompt prefers?
D2. The run-singer title shows "Run #109 · Pentatonic runs · C major · Tenor"

Problem: The title reads as one fixed run, but the exercise moves up a semitone each pass.
Proposal: Show the current pass and its key ("Pass 2 of 5 · D♭ major"), or the note range. Drop the run number.
Acceptance: The title always matches what is currently playing.
D3. Voice exercises have no progression display in play-along

Problem: The guide-tones exercise uses a play-along as accompaniment, but it shows no chord progression the way strum-along does. The play-along component is also built for guitar playing.
Proposal: Add a chord-progression strip (current chord lit, one per bar) to a play-along used as accompaniment. Or add a separate "chords" tool for singers.
Acceptance: A singer sees which chord is playing and what comes next. The text no longer says "play along".
